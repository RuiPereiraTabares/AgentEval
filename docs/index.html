Grounding measures how well the AI's response is backed by the cited article. A cited statement can be traced to specific content in the article; an uncited statement is an AI assertion with no article support — a potential hallucination risk. The Good Citation Rate shows what percentage of all evaluated citations were considered accurate and grounded in the source material. Grounding Verdicts classify each case: Well Grounded (most claims supported) → Partially → Poorly → Ungrounded (response not traceable to the article).
